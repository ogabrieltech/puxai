# PuxAI

PuxAI is the branded product layer built on top of the Chatwoot open-source core.

## Brand

- Product: PuxAI
- Primary: `#7C3AED`
- Accent: `#A78BFA`
- Direction: dark-first, clean and minimal

## Current scope

This first phase is intentionally limited to product branding and visual identity. The underlying customer support core remains intact. AI agents, bots, WhatsApp setup, automations and other product-specific integrations are intentionally deferred to later phases.
